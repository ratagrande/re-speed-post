Frequently asked questions

Does 1.00× modify or re-encode my video?

No. At exactly 1.00×, ReSpeedPost uses the selected source file unchanged and does not start FFmpeg.

Where does video processing happen?

On your computer. ReSpeedPost does not upload videos to its OAuth service. When you confirm a post, the resulting local file uploads directly from your device to TikTok.

Why are only some privacy options shown?

ReSpeedPost displays the privacy options returned by TikTok for the connected creator at that moment. It does not invent or override unavailable settings.

Can I edit the caption before publishing?

Yes. Captions and hashtags are editable in both the UI and CLI workflow before final confirmation.

Why is a post still processing?

TikTok may need several minutes to process a completed upload. ReSpeedPost polls the official post-status endpoint and reports completion or the failure reason returned by TikTok.

How do I disconnect TikTok?

Remove ReSpeedPost from TikTok's connected apps settings, then delete %APPDATA%\ReSpeedPost\tiktok_token.json from your computer.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Confirm FFmpeg and FFprobe are installed and available on PATH.
  • Confirm the selected file contains a supported video stream.
  • Refresh creator information immediately before posting.
  • Use only a visibility option returned for the connected creator.
  • Check TikTok account restrictions and daily posting limits.
  • Do not retry a post repeatedly while TikTok still reports processing.

Contact

For product questions or reproducible defects, use the ReSpeedPost issue tracker. Do not include access tokens, OAuth codes, private videos, passwords, or other secrets in reports.
